\# 💙 Task 2 - MI FanZone Website 💙



Welcome to the MI FanZone – a fan-made website dedicated to the most successful franchise in IPL history, the Mumbai Indians! 🏏



As a passionate Mumbai Indians supporter and a huge fan of Rohit Sharma, I created this website to celebrate the team's achievements, legendary players, unforgettable moments, and the incredible MI fan community.

\- 👥 Players Page

\- 📅 Match Schedule

\- 📊 Team Statistics

\- 🖼️ Gallery

\- 📞 Contact Page
